# Art prompt pack: v8

The wrong side of the law: thirteen card scenes for small cheats, a trial and prison, and three
badges. The trial itself uses `card.courtroom`, already drawn. Four sheets.

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`). Attach
`art/raw/cards/cards-24.png` as the style reference, start each message with
`Match the attached sheet's style and level of detail exactly.`, and paste the **redraw note** from
`v4-prompt-pack.md` before each card prompt: people with faces, one clear moment, one prop that
tells it. Keep the humour: these are comic scenes, never grim. The checks before saving are the same
as in v1.

## 88. Accused, inside, and out

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-31.png` — `card.crime_wrongly_accused`, `card.crime_jail_cellmate`, `card.crime_jail_commissary`, `card.crime_jail_library`, `card.crime_memoirs`, `card.crime_fake_mba`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Wrongly accused: a bewildered person in a police interview room, holding up a dentist's appointment card as an alibi, a detective across the table raising one sceptical eyebrow, a desk lamp between them.
2. A cellmate who was a banker: a prison cell with bunk beds, an older inmate in an orange jumpsuit on the top bunk drawing stock charts on the wall with chalk, the player below in the same jumpsuit taking notes in a small notebook.
3. The noodle economy: a prison corridor, an inmate with a trolley stacked high with instant noodle cups trading them like money, other inmates queuing with little handfuls of cups, a guard pretending not to see.
4. The prison library: a small library behind bars, the player in an orange jumpsuit reading a thick dictionary at a wooden table, a tower of books beside them, a barred window with a sliver of sky.
5. Memoirs from a cell: a proud ex-prisoner at a book signing in a bookshop, a stack of books with a barred-window cover design as blank shapes, a short queue of fans holding copies.
6. A small upgrade: an employee at a laptop late at night, typing "MBA" into an online CV as blank shapes, a fake framed diploma propped against the screen with a glue stick beside it, a guilty smile.
```

## 89. Small cheats

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-32.png` — `card.crime_fake_mba_found`, `card.crime_ham_smuggling`, `card.crime_memecoin`, `card.crime_illegal_betting`, `card.crime_enchufe`, `card.crime_insider_tip`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. HR has a question: a nervous employee sweating in a glass meeting room, a stern HR manager holding up an empty certificate frame, a clock on the wall pointing to Friday as a simple icon.
2. Ham smuggling: an airport customs desk, an officer lifting a whole cured ham out of an open suitcase full of more hams, a sniffer dog wagging its tail, the traveller smiling far too innocently.
3. Launching a meme coin: a young person filming themselves at a desk with a ring light, a big gold coin with a rat's face on the screen, a rocket-shaped chart shooting up, phones of fans glowing around.
4. A sure bet: a smoky sports bar at night, a shady man in a flat cap whispering behind his hand to the player, a football match on a television behind them, betting slips on the bar.
5. A cousin in the ministry: a grand government corridor, a smug cousin in a suit slipping an official envelope to the player, a rubber stamp and a queue of real applicants with folders watching suspiciously.
6. Your brother-in-law knows something: a family barbecue in a garden, a brother-in-law in an apron leaning in to whisper to the player, holding a phone that shows a rising share chart, the rest of the family laughing in the background.
```
