## 97. Red tape

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-35.png` — `card.red_licence`, `card.red_appointment`, `card.red_works_stopped`, `card.red_inheritance`, `card.red_grant`, `card.refuge_bank_freeze`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. No licence yet: a small bar owner standing outside a shuttered bar with a "coming soon" sign as a blank shape, holding a thick folder of forms, an inspector with a clipboard shaking his head.
2. The appointment: a long queue of tired people in a grey government office, a ticket machine showing a very high number, the player holding ticket number 1 and a coffee, a clock on the wall.
3. Works stopped: a half-finished kitchen renovation with tools left lying, a bright official notice taped across the doorway, the player and a builder staring at it.
4. The inheritance in limbo: the player at a notary's desk under a mountain of stamped papers, an old framed photo of a smiling aunt on the desk, the notary pushing yet another form across.
5. The grant: the player at a home desk at night facing a stack of forms forty pages thick, a laptop showing an error window as blank shapes, a cold cup of tea.
6. The bank freeze: a queue outside a bank with its shutters half down and a notice on the door as blank shapes, worried people holding bank cards, and one calm person at the back holding a small gold bar and a phone.
```
