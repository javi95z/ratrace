New ways to make a living: living from a business, a lodger in the spare room, and making something
that pays royalties. Five card scenes and two badges. Two sheets.

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`). Attach

## 93. Lodgers and a comeback

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-34.png` — `card.lodger_good`, `card.lodger_unpaid`, `card.lodger_friend`, `card.lodger_tension`, `card.work_revival`, `card.work_plagiarism`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. The perfect lodger: a tidy young lodger handing the player an envelope of rent in a hallway, a spotless shared kitchen behind them, a little potted plant on the shelf.
2. The lodger who does not pay: the player knocking on a closed bedroom door, holding an empty envelope, loud music notes coming from under the door, a pile of unwashed dishes in the sink.
3. A lodger who becomes a friend: the player and the lodger laughing at a kitchen table late at night, the lodger introducing the player to a well-dressed guest holding a business card.
4. Tension at home: a partner with arms crossed in a doorway glaring at the lodger, who eats cereal in pyjamas on the family sofa, the player caught in the middle with a nervous smile.
5. Back in fashion: a bookshop window with a big "back in stock" banner as a blank shape over a stack of the player's old book, a queue of young readers, the player peeking in surprised.
6. Copied: the player in a living room staring at a television showing an advert with suspiciously familiar artwork, holding their own book up next to the screen to compare.
```

Landscape image, 2:1. Object sheet: 2 columns by 1 row, 2 badges. Every badge is a round medallion of identical size with a dark navy enamel centre, a gold rim, and one simple bold symbol in the centre. Left to right:
