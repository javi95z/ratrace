# Art prompt pack: v9

New ways to make a living: a lodger in the spare room, and living from a business. Four card scenes,
one badge, and the five kinds of business drawn properly at 64×64 (today they are 32-pixel icons
blown up, which looks coarse beside everything else). Three sheets.

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`). Attach
`art/raw/cards/cards-24.png` as the style reference, start each message with
`Match the attached sheet's style and level of detail exactly.`, and paste the **redraw note** from
`v4-prompt-pack.md` before each card prompt: people with faces, one clear moment, one prop that
tells it. Keep the humour. The checks before saving are the same as in v1.

## 93. Lodgers

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-34.png` — `card.lodger_good`, `card.lodger_unpaid`, `card.lodger_friend`, `card.lodger_tension`

```text
Landscape 2:1 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 2 rows, 4 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. The perfect lodger: a tidy young lodger handing the player an envelope of rent in a hallway, a spotless shared kitchen behind them, a little potted plant on the shelf.
2. The lodger who does not pay: the player knocking on a closed bedroom door, holding an empty envelope, loud music notes coming from under the door, a pile of unwashed dishes in the sink.
3. A lodger who becomes a friend: the player and the lodger laughing at a kitchen table late at night, the lodger introducing the player to a well-dressed guest holding a business card.
4. Tension at home: a partner with arms crossed in a doorway glaring at the lodger, who eats cereal in pyjamas on the family sofa, the player caught in the middle with a nervous smile.
```

## 95. Five businesses

Save as `art/raw/items/business-01.png` — `business.services`, `business.shop`, `business.app`, `business.bar`, `business.franchise`

Attach `art/raw/items/items-01.png`.

```text
Landscape image, 5:1. Object sheet: 5 columns by 1 row, 5 objects, each shown front-on and centred in its cell. Left to right:
1. Your trade on your own: a small workshop door with a hand-painted sign, a toolbox and a laptop on a stool beside it.
2. An online shop: a cardboard parcel with a shopping-cart sticker, a laptop behind it showing a product page as blank shapes.
3. An app: a smartphone standing upright, its screen showing a bright app icon and a rising bar chart.
4. A bar: a small bar front with a striped awning, two stools and a beer tap on the counter.
5. A franchise: a fast-food shop front with a big generic logo shape, a drive-through window and a queue of little cars.
```
