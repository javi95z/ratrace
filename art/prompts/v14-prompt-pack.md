# Art prompt pack: v14

Six sheets: the rat in eight poses, what a player's rat can wear, a backdrop for each city behind the portraits, six scenes of a
life's milestones, every business at three sizes, and a map of the world at night.

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`). Each sheet below
says which approved sheet to attach as its style reference. Start every message with
`Match the attached sheet's style and level of detail exactly.` The checks before saving are the
same as in v1.

## 99. The rat, in eight poses

Save as `art/raw/brand/brand-02.png` — `rat.cheer`, `rat.hide`, `rat.exit`, `rat.count`, `rat.sleep`, `rat.shock`, `rat.think`, `rat.wave`

Attach `art/raw/brand/brand-01.webp` (the mascot is its fourth object): the rat must be the very same character.

```text
Landscape image, 2:1. Object sheet: 4 columns by 2 rows, 8 objects. Every object is the same cartoon rat as the mascot in the attached sheet — same face, same grey fur, same pink ears and tail, same navy business suit and gold tie — shown full body, the same size in every cell. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Jumping for joy, both arms up, a few gold coins bursting above its head.
2. Crouching behind a small stack of newspapers, only its eyes and ears showing, worried.
3. Walking out through an open doorway full of warm golden light, seen from behind, a small suitcase in one hand.
4. Sitting cross-legged counting a small pile of gold coins, one coin held up to its eye.
5. Asleep curled on a small cushion, a tiny nightcap on, a speech-bubble-free pose with closed eyes.
6. Shocked, fur standing on end, mouth open, a falling red arrow chart on a small sign beside it.
7. Thinking, one paw on its chin, looking up, a small gold lightbulb floating above.
8. Waving hello with one paw, smiling, the other paw holding its tie.
```

## 100. A backdrop for each city

Save as `art/raw/portraits/backdrops-01.png` — `backdrop.madrid`, `backdrop.london`, `backdrop.new_york`, `backdrop.dubai`, `backdrop.singapore`, `backdrop.bangkok`, `backdrop.tokyo`, `backdrop.moscow`, `backdrop.mexico_city`, `backdrop.zurich`

Attach `art/raw/skylines/` (any approved night skyline sheet). These sit behind a portrait, so they must be quiet: the face goes on top.

```text
Landscape image, 5:2. Scene sheet: 5 columns by 2 rows, 10 square panels separated by magenta gutters. Each panel is a calm square backdrop for a character portrait: a soft night sky in one dominant colour, with a low, simple, dark silhouette of the city's most famous landmark along the bottom quarter only, and a few stars. No people, nothing in the centre of the panel. Each city has its own sky colour so that they tell apart at a glance. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Madrid: warm terracotta sky, the Metropolis building dome and the Puerta de Alcalá.
2. London: cool teal sky, Big Ben and the London Eye.
3. New York: deep blue sky, the Empire State and Chrysler buildings.
4. Dubai: golden amber sky, the Burj Khalifa and the Burj Al Arab.
5. Singapore: emerald sky, the Marina Bay Sands and a Supertree.
6. Bangkok: orange dusk sky, the spires of Wat Arun.
7. Tokyo: crimson-red sky, Tokyo Tower and a pagoda.
8. Moscow: icy pale-blue sky, the onion domes of St Basil's.
9. Mexico City: warm pink-coral sky (not magenta), the Angel of Independence column.
10. Zurich: soft sky blue, the twin towers of the Grossmünster and a mountain ridge.
```
