# Art prompt pack: v3

One sheet for what the game has grown since v2: a sign for each kind of business, a sign for each skill and for money sense, and the few signs the second-hand shop and the comparison at the end of a life use.

Use it exactly as the v1 pack: the same ChatGPT project, the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`), and the approved sheet 01 attached to every request, starting the message with `Match the attached sheet's style exactly.` The checks before saving are the same.

Nothing in the game waits on it: today a business uses `icon.business`, and each skill borrows a career's sign.
