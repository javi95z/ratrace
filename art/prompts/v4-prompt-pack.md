# Art prompt pack: v4

Seven card sheets redrawn: `cards-09` to `cards-15`, the work, deals, tax, investing, housing, homes
and learning scenes of the first pack. They were drawn under the v1 rule that people are "small
generic figures", and it shows: faceless silhouettes, scenes that say little, and abstract props
that could be anything. The sheets drawn since (`cards-22` to `cards-25`) have people with faces,
one clear action and a prop that tells the story at a glance; these are redrawn to match them.

Every scene keeps its sprite id and its place on the sheet, so nothing in the game changes but the
picture.

## How to use

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`), with two changes:

1. Attach `art/raw/cards/cards-24.png` as the style reference instead of sheet 01, and start each
   message with: `Match the attached sheet's style and level of detail exactly.`
2. Paste the **redraw note** below before the sheet prompt. It overrides the style block's rule
   about small generic figures.

Save each result over the old file, under the same name. The checks before saving are the same as in v1.

All seven redrawn and wired.

## Redraw note

```text
Redraw note, overriding the style block where they differ:
- People are characters at medium size with readable faces and clear expressions (worried, proud, smug, tired), never faceless silhouettes or shadow figures.
- Each panel tells one moment: one main character doing one clear thing, and one prop that makes the story obvious at a glance. Nothing vague or symbolic floating in the air.
- Warm interior light against the cool night outside, as in the attached sheet. Rich detail in the foreground, simpler background.
- Screens, papers and signs still show only abstract shapes, bars and icons, never letters or numbers, unless a panel says otherwise.
```
