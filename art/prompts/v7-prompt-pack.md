# Art prompt pack: v7

Twenty card scenes, two for each of the ten cities: what each city is like to live in. Four sheets.

Use the same ChatGPT project and the same **style block** (in `v1-prompt-pack.md`). Attach
`art/raw/cards/cards-24.png` as the style reference, start each message with
`Match the attached sheet's style and level of detail exactly.`, and paste the **redraw note** from
`v4-prompt-pack.md` before the prompt: people with faces, one clear moment, one prop that tells it.
The checks before saving are the same as in v1.

## 84. Madrid, London, New York

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-27.png` — `card.city_madrid_cana`, `card.city_madrid_tourist_flat`, `card.city_london_tube_strike`, `card.city_london_club`, `card.city_new_york_rent_hike`, `card.city_new_york_elevator_pitch`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Madrid: a lively tapas bar at night, two colleagues in work clothes laughing at the counter with small glasses of beer, plates of tapas, hams hanging above, one checking a wristwatch that already says late.
2. Madrid: an empty sunny flat in August, a suitcase by the open door, a tourist couple with a map being handed keys by the owner, a fan turning, the city baking outside.
3. London: a crowded Underground station entrance with its shutters closed and a strike sign as a blank shape, a commuter in a raincoat staring at a phone, a queue for black cabs behind.
4. London: a leather-armchaired private club room, a member in a tweed jacket introducing a slightly nervous newcomer to a group by the fireplace, portraits on the wall.
5. New York: a tenant in a small apartment holding a letter with a big red upward arrow, a fire escape outside the window, boxes of takeout on the table.
6. New York: inside a lift with a brass panel, a young professional talking eagerly with their hands to a silver-haired investor in a long coat who glances at a watch, the floor numbers as dots climbing.
```

## 85. Dubai, Singapore, Bangkok

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-28.png` — `card.city_dubai_visa`, `card.city_dubai_golden_visa`, `card.city_singapore_ballot`, `card.city_singapore_gum`, `card.city_bangkok_flood`, `card.city_bangkok_beach_bar`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Dubai: a busy government service hall with numbered ticket screens as dots, a tired resident holding a passport and a stack of forms, rows of waiting seats, cool marble.
2. Dubai: a proud resident receiving a gold card across a polished desk from an official, the Burj Khalifa visible through floor-to-ceiling windows.
3. Singapore: a family crowded around a laptop at home, fingers crossed, a lottery-style ballot screen with spinning number balls, new public housing towers through the window.
4. Singapore: a spotless train platform, a stern warden in uniform writing a ticket for an embarrassed commuter who has gum, a no-gum sign as a crossed-out symbol.
5. Bangkok: a flooded street of shophouses in monsoon rain, a resident in rubber boots piling sandbags at their door, motorbikes half underwater, lanterns reflected in the water.
6. Bangkok: a beach bar of bamboo and thatch at sunset, two friends shaking hands behind the counter, string lights, hammocks and the sea.
```

## 86. Tokyo, Moscow, Mexico City

Save as `art/raw/cards/cards-29.png` — `card.city_tokyo_earthquake`, `card.city_tokyo_karaoke`, `card.city_moscow_rouble`, `card.city_moscow_dacha`, `card.city_mexico_city_mordida`, `card.city_mexico_city_taqueria`

```text
Landscape 3:2 image. Scene sheet: 2 columns by 3 rows, 6 panels. Left to right, top to bottom:
1. Tokyo: inside a small apartment just after an earthquake, a resident crouched under a table looking up at a swinging lamp, books on the floor, a crack in the wall, motion lines on the shelves.
2. Tokyo: a karaoke room glowing with neon, a nervous employee holding the microphone, the boss in a loosened tie cheering with a raised glass, colleagues clapping.
3. Moscow: an exchange office window at night with a big red falling arrow on its rate board, a worried office worker counting a thin wad of notes, snow on the pavement outside.
4. Moscow: a wooden dacha with carved window frames in a birch wood, a smiling heir holding an old key on the porch, a vegetable patch and a small smoking banya hut.
5. Mexico City: an inspector in a cap holding a clipboard at a small shop counter, his hand open and waiting, the shop owner hesitating with a folded banknote, papel picado above.
6. Mexico City: a taco stand at night with a glowing trompo of meat, the taquero in an apron shaking hands with a new partner, a queue of happy customers.
```
