# Rooms Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two to six friends live the same life in the same world at the same time, a round at a time, with a clock on every round, and see who does best with it (roadmap step 15).

**Architecture:** A room is rows in four tables and one Edge Function, `rooms`, that plays every member's life the way `ranked` plays one: from a seed it keeps and a log, with the engine of the room's versions. Cards are played at once; a round's orders wait in `room_orders` until the round turns for everyone, in one database function that holds the room's row. Pages hear of changes through Realtime Postgres changes and ask the function how the room stands. On the device a room's life gets its own round store, shown on the same play screens through a scoped game context, so the device's own life in progress is never touched.

**Tech Stack:** Supabase Postgres (plpgsql functions, row-level security, Realtime publication), Supabase Edge Functions (Deno, `npm:@supabase/server`), `@supabase/supabase-js` Realtime, Next.js 16 / React 19 / zustand, vitest + Testing Library, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-ratrace-online-design.md`, §6 (tables), §7 "Rooms", §11 decisions 22 to 31.

## Global Constraints

- Every room table has row-level security; `authenticated` is granted only `select` on `rooms` and `room_players`, filtered to members; `room_orders` has no grant and no policy. Nothing is granted to `anon`.
- The database functions that change a room are `security definer`, `set search_path = ''`, executable by `service_role` only.
- The live project has real players: tests never count or clear whole tables; the live check removes only the guests it made.
- A room is opened only on `CURRENT` (the rules the game plays now) and with a seed of 128 bits or more; joined only by a page on the room's versions.
- Rate limits: `room-step` 240 a minute (every call about a room in play), `room-make` 20 an hour (opening and joining).
- Seconds a round: 90, 180 (default), 300. Grace after the deadline: 5 seconds. Six members at most, two to start. A room untouched for 24 hours is closed when next looked at.
- Text in English, Spanish and Russian, to `docs/i18n/russian.md` for the Russian. UI: simplest version, details in a `Sheet`, no framed box inside another, hide what cannot be used.
- Work lands on `dev` only; `main` is never touched. Functions are deployed with `pnpm online:deploy`, which from now on deploys `rooms` too.

## Review Focus

1. Two members who choose the same in a room end with the same life, figure for figure, and the same as a ranked life on the same seed would: the server's fill of unanswered cards and empty orders must be exactly what the engine does.
2. Races at the deadline: a card or orders that reach the server while the round is turning, two pages turning the same round at once, a turn whose write fails half way. Nothing may be played twice, lost, or played for the wrong round.
3. Nothing about another member's round leaks before it turns: not through `room_players`, not through Realtime, not through the function's answers.
4. A member who closes the page mid-round, or whose phone sleeps through several rounds, comes back to the room as it stands, told so, and can play on.
5. The device's own life in progress (casual or ranked) is untouched by everything a room does, and the mirror of cloud saves neither uploads nor offers a room's life.
